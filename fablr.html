Project Overview

High-Fidelity Book Matchmaking

Fablr is designed to solve Book Paralysis. Built for genre-fiction enthusiasts (Webnovels, Manga, Fantasy), it uses a Hook-First discovery model that prioritizes narrative DNA over misleading cover art.
