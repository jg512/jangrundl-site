Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 7, 2026

Welcome to fablr. Your privacy is a core priority for us. This policy outlines how we handle your data to provide the best book matchmaking experience possible.

1. Information We Collect

To provide personalized recommendations (Taste DNA), we collect the following:

  • Account Data: Email address and username provided during Sign Up.
  • Interaction Data: Books you swipe, save, "like," or "love." This is used solely to train your local recommendation engine.
  • Community Data: Messages and posts you share in fablr chatrooms.
  • Push Tokens: A unique ID for your device to send you updates (only if you grant permission).

2. How Your Data is Used

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To generate your Taste DNA and book matches.
  • To enable social features like the Literary Circle and Friend Reading Rooms.
  • To maintain your Reading Streak and Daily Challenge progress.
  • To improve the app's performance and fix technical bugs.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell your personal data. Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL). We only share data when required by law or to provide the service.

4. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You may:

  • Access/Export: View your library and Taste DNA within the app.
  • Delete: You can permanently delete your account and all associated data via the Settings > Delete Account menu inside the app. This action is instantaneous and irreversible.
